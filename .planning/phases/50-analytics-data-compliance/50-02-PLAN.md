**fact-aggregation.service.ts (FactAggregationService):**
- Inject FactCaseService, FactRiuService, FactCampaignService
- Inject @InjectQueue('fact-aggregation') queue
- `queueIncrementalUpdate(entityType: 'case' | 'riu' | 'campaign', entityId: string)`: Add job to queue with retry options (attempts: 3, backoff exponential)
- `queueFullReconciliation(organizationId: string)`: Add full rebuild job
- `scheduleNightlyReconciliation()`: Register repeating job with cron '0 2 * * *' (2 AM daily)

**fact-aggregation.processor.ts (FactAggregationProcessor):**

- @Processor('fact-aggregation') decorator
- Extend WorkerHost
- Handle 'incremental-update' jobs: Call appropriate service.aggregateForEntity
- Handle 'full-reconciliation' jobs: For each organization, rebuild all facts for last 30 days
- concurrency: 3 to avoid overwhelming database

**jobs.module.ts:**

Follow existing processor patterns (see scheduled-export.processor.ts).
